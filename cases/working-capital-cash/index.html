D / TREASURY · WORKING CAPITAL

13-week cash flow & working-capital model

Will liquidity remain above the operating guardrail—and which customer, supplier and inventory actions protect the next 13 weeks?

DECISION BRIEF

The selected Base case exhausts a $750k facility in week 2 and turns cash negative in week 5, making weekly ownership more important than the closing balance alone.

THE LIQUIDITY QUESTION

Make the cash gap visible early enough to act.

Northstar Distribution is a synthetic multi-category distributor with $3.25M of opening receivables, $2.10M of payables and $1.65M of inventory. The model connects every opening balance to weekly cash timing.

The objective is not to force a positive forecast. It is to identify the first guardrail breach, quantify the funding requirement and make collections, supplier and inventory actions explicit.

01 / TIMINGWEEK 3

The guardrail breaks before cash turns negative.

Management receives an early warning two weeks before the first negative-cash week.

02 / FUNDING$750K

The approved facility is not enough.

The line is fully drawn in week 2, leaving no additional capacity against later operating outflows.

03 / WORKING CAPITAL$672K

Cash release helps, but does not close the gap.

Collections and inventory actions release cash while faster supplier payments offset part of the benefit.

MODEL ARCHITECTURE

Collect. Pay. Protect liquidity.

  1. 01

    Collect

    Customer balance, due week, collectability and scenario timing create a named weekly AR plan.

  2. 02

    Pay

    Vendor schedules distinguish critical suppliers from payments that can be managed within an agreed stance.

  3. 03

    Protect

    Inventory deferrals, operating commitments and facility capacity roll into ending cash and headroom every week.

MANAGEMENT READOUT

Start a weekly liquidity war room before week 3—not after the cash balance turns negative.

Assign named collection owners, protect critical suppliers, defer purchases only against verified excess stock, and secure incremental funding or operating actions before the existing facility is exhausted.
