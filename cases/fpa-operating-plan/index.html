Operating plan & rolling forecast

Where will FY2026 land versus budget, which drivers explain the gap, and what actions protect liquidity?

THE MANAGEMENT QUESTION

Turn operating drivers into one defendable outlook.

AtlasCloud is a synthetic B2B SaaS business with six closed months and a twelve-month rolling view. The model connects product-level units and pricing to hiring, functional spend, EBITDA, and liquidity.

The objective is not simply to produce a budget. It is to explain where FY2026 will land, isolate the drivers behind the gap, and show which action management can take without breaching the cash guardrail.

01 / REVENUE$30.6M

Almost on budget.

The FY2026 base case is only $15K below budget, with the monthly plan tied back to product volume and price.

02 / PROFIT$8.8M

Operating choices stay visible.

A 28.6% EBITDA margin reflects the combined effect of commercial assumptions, headcount, and functional opex.

03 / LIQUIDITY$25.5M

Cash remains the guardrail.

The roll-forward converts operating performance into ending cash and tests every month against a $3.0M minimum.

MODEL ARCHITECTURE

Drivers first. Outputs second.

  1. 01

    Build revenue

    Product volume × realized or forecast price creates the commercial plan and separates actual from forecast months.

  2. 02

    Connect resources

    Hiring, attrition, compensation, and departmental opex flow into the monthly P&L.

  3. 03

    Stress and control

    Central scenario multipliers update growth, margin, and cash while six checks protect reconciliation integrity.

MANAGEMENT READOUT

Keep the base plan, but manage the business through driver variance and liquidity—not the annual total alone.

Refresh actual units and prices monthly, challenge hiring and opex assumptions with owners, and distribute only when every control shows PASS.
