Power BI business performance dashboard

How can management move from a high-level P&L view to the drivers behind performance, segment contribution, and break-even dynamics?

DECISION BRIEF

A four-page report moves from executive KPIs to detailed drivers, segment comparisons, and break-even analysis through one interactive decision flow.

THE MANAGEMENT QUESTION

Keep the route from result to driver visible.

The report is organized as a management review rather than a gallery of charts. The opening page establishes direction, then the next three pages progressively expose drivers, segment contribution, and profitability thresholds.

The published PBIX is the evidence. The portfolio page explains the analytical flow without claiming business outcomes that the report does not verify.

REVIEW SEQUENCE

From headline to management follow-up.

  1. 01

    Orient

    Use Overview to establish the overall P&L direction and choose where to investigate.

  2. 02

    Diagnose

    Move through Breakdown and Segments to isolate the components and contributions behind the headline.

  3. 03

    Test the threshold

    Use Breakeven to inspect profitability behavior before defining the next management question.
