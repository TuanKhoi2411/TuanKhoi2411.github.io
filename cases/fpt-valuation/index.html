THE CHALLENGE

Convert a strong operating story into a value range.

FPT reported VND 70,113bn of revenue and VND 15,958bn of EBITDA in 2025. The model converts official historical highlights into a five-year operating forecast, unlevered free cash flow, and a transparent enterprise-to-equity bridge.

The decision is not whether one point estimate is “right.” It is which assumptions create the value and how quickly that view changes.

01 / GROWTH12.0%

Growth moderates through 2030.

The base case steps revenue growth from 14% in 2026E to 10% in 2030E.

02 / MARGIN23.5%

Modest EBITDA expansion.

Margin increases gradually as scale benefits offset continued strategic investment.

03 / VALUEVND 122K

A model output, not a price target.

Per-share value depends directly on WACC, terminal growth, and the illustrative net-cash bridge.

MODEL ARCHITECTURE

Drivers before outputs.

  1. 01

    Normalize

    Anchor historical performance to official disclosures and flag the 2026 accounting comparability change.

  2. 02

    Forecast

    Link growth, margin, capex, working capital, and tax assumptions to unlevered cash flow.

  3. 03

    Stress

    Recompute every sensitivity cell from WACC and terminal growth; run six audit controls.

VALUATION VIEW

The base case is attractive only if double-digit growth and gradual margin expansion survive the accounting transition.

Net cash remains an illustrative input and should be replaced with audited debt and cash detail before any live-use conclusion.
