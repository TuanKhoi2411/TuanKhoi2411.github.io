Data pipeline & executive P&L

How can fragmented monthly finance files become a refreshable P&L that management can trust?

DECISION BRIEF

A controlled pipeline standardizes 5,832 records, maps them into a star schema, and keeps executive metrics tied to visible data-quality checks.

Toolkit

THE REPORTING PROBLEM

Numbers cannot guide action until the pipeline earns trust.

This portfolio case uses a synthetic FY2026 dataset; it does not represent employer or client records. Monthly finance files arrive with transaction, account, entity, cost-center, period, scenario, and currency fields. Before management sees a dashboard, the workflow has to standardize types, map dimensions, reject broken records, and preserve a route back to source.

The management question is broader than “what is EBITDA?” It is which entity or cost driver explains the variance—and whether the data is controlled enough to distribute.

01 / GROWTH+0.8%

Revenue finishes ahead of budget.

The dashboard reports $28.9M of FY2026 revenue and directs attention to the entities that sustain or dilute the result.

02 / PROFITABILITY21.4%

Margin is a managed outcome.

Gross margin and people-cost drivers remain visible beside EBITDA rather than being buried in a consolidated total.

03 / CONTROL5,832

Every record is accounted for.

Row count, IDs, mappings, scenario coverage, and consolidated tie-out all pass before the pack is distributed.

PIPELINE ARCHITECTURE

One metric language from file to decision.

  1. 01

    Standardize

    Power Query types, cleans, maps, and validates repeatable monthly CSV inputs.

  2. 02

    Model

    A finance fact table joins account, entity, cost-center, and date dimensions through a governed star schema.

  3. 03

    Explain

    Shared Actual, Budget, Forecast, PY, variance, and margin measures feed Excel and Power BI without conflicting definitions.

MANAGEMENT READOUT

Protect strong revenue, investigate structural cost variance, and never separate the dashboard from its controls.

Replace the source files, refresh Power Query and the semantic model, confirm PASS, then investigate the largest entity, margin, and opex movements.
