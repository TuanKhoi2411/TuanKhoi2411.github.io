Business background

The dataset models a sports-and-health manufacturer and distributor through 580 financial transactions in 2023. A star schema—one fact table plus six supporting dimensions—connects revenue, COGS, Opex, interest and tax across Sports Equipment, Sportswear and Nutrition & Food Supplements.

Decision objective: sustain profit across business lines, control COGS and Opex, and allocate resources according to margin, EBIT and resilience evidence.

WHY 01

Sportswear produced $2.737M net profit on $6.808M revenue; its 40.2% net margin is 14.5 points above Sports equipment.

WHY 02

Nutrition contributed 10.5% of revenue but lost $0.713M, with a -38.7% net margin that diluted both profitable lines.

SO WHAT

Scale Sportswear selectively and put Nutrition behind a margin-recovery gate before funding further growth.

02 / SEASONAL RESILIENCE

September was not just the lowest month—net margin collapsed to 8.2%.

WHAT
WHY
$91.6KSEPTEMBER NET PROFIT
WHY 01

Revenue fell to $1.121M, 42.5% below January. Net profit dropped to $91.6K, leaving only an 8.2% margin.

WHY 02

Sports equipment lost $15.1K in September while Nutrition lost $56.6K; December then recovered to a 68.0% gross margin.

SO WHAT

Treat September as a designed resilience period: adjust product mix, cost commitments and working plans before the trough.

04 / BREAKEVEN RESILIENCE

Every month cleared breakeven, but September's cushion narrowed to $0.278M.

WHAT
WHY
1.94×ANNUAL REVENUE / BREAKEVEN
WHY 01

Annual revenue was $17.560M against $9.072M breakeven revenue, creating an $8.488M annual cushion.

WHY 02

The monthly cushion ranged from $1.223M in January to only $0.278M in September—77.3% less protection.

Use the September cushion as the operating stress threshold and trigger cost or mix actions before the buffer falls below $0.30M.

WHY

EXECUTIVE SYNTHESIS

Four connections define the financial operating system.

01 / PORTFOLIO × PROFIT

Sportswear is the economic engine.

38.8% of revenue creates 63.4% of net profit; capital allocation should follow margin quality, not size alone.

02 / SEASON × PORTFOLIO

September exposes weak lines.

The annual trough combined a Sports equipment loss with a continuing Nutrition loss, compressing group net margin to 8.2%.

03 / COST × RESILIENCE

Fixed concentration narrows the cushion.

Labor and three Opex pools dominate costs, so the $0.278M September buffer can erode quickly without active controls.

04 / OPEX × EBIT SCALE

Cost discipline determines scale.

Q1 paired 28.6% Opex intensity with a 36.5% EBIT margin; Q3 moved to 34.9% and 21.6%, proving that revenue alone does not preserve earnings.
