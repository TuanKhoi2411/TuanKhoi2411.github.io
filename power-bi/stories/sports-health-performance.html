Business background

The dataset models a sports-and-health manufacturer and distributor through 580 financial transactions in 2023. The semantic model links one fact table to four business dimensions—Calendar, Business Line, Group and Subgroup—supported by dedicated measure and helper tables.

Evidence boundary: this deck uses the published 2023 Power BI model and its 580 transactions. No external market benchmark or causal claim is added.

01 / PORTFOLIO ECONOMICS

Sportswear generated 63.4% of net profit from 38.8% of revenue.

PORTFOLIO SCALE
MARGIN QUALITY
40.2% SPORTSWEAR NET MARGIN
EVIDENCE / PROFIT QUALITY

Sportswear produced $2.737M net profit on $6.808M revenue. Its 40.2% net margin is 14.5 points above Sports equipment, so the smaller revenue line creates the strongest earnings conversion.

EVIDENCE / PORTFOLIO DRAG

Nutrition contributed only 10.5% of revenue yet lost $0.713M. Its -38.7% net margin is a structural drag, not a scale advantage waiting to emerge.

DECISION / CAPITAL ALLOCATION

Protect Sportswear capacity and pricing. Put every incremental Nutrition investment behind a product-level path to positive contribution; growth without that gate would dilute the portfolio.

02 / SEASONAL RESILIENCE

September was not just the lowest month—net margin collapsed to 8.2%.

MONTHLY PERFORMANCE
MARGIN CONVERSION
$91.6K SEPTEMBER NET PROFIT
EVIDENCE / TROUGH DEPTH

September revenue fell to $1.121M, 42.5% below January. Net profit compressed to $91.6K and an 8.2% margin, making the trough economically material.

EVIDENCE / MIX EFFECT

The group trough contained a $15.1K Sports equipment loss and a $56.6K Nutrition loss. December's 68.0% gross margin shows the weakness was concentrated, not permanent.

DECISION / SEASONAL PLAYBOOK

Pre-set September mix, capacity and discretionary-spend actions during the Q2 forecast. Escalate when revenue and margin fall together instead of waiting for the monthly close.

03 / COST ARCHITECTURE

Labor is 66.9% of COGS; three lines absorb 74.5% of Opex.

COST MIX
COST CONCENTRATION
$4.49M LABOR COGS
EVIDENCE / COGS

COGS was $6.712M; Labor alone was $4.492M, or 66.9%. Unit economics therefore depend heavily on labor productivity and deployment.

EVIDENCE / OPEX

Payroll, Equipment and Marketing totaled $4.178M—74.5% of Opex. A small number of owners therefore control most of the available operating leverage.

DECISION / OWNER CONTROL

Assign labor productivity, payroll capacity, equipment utilization and marketing return to named owners. Review variance and benefit together so cuts do not damage productive capacity.

04 / BREAKEVEN RESILIENCE

Every month cleared breakeven, but September's cushion narrowed to $0.278M.

BREAKEVEN TREND
CUSHION STRESS
1.94× ANNUAL REVENUE / BREAKEVEN
EVIDENCE / ANNUAL VIEW

Annual revenue was $17.560M against $9.072M breakeven revenue, creating an $8.488M cushion. The full year is resilient in aggregate.

EVIDENCE / MONTHLY STRESS

The cushion narrowed from $1.223M in January to $0.278M in September—77.3% less protection. The annual ratio therefore hides a meaningful seasonal risk.

DECISION / EARLY WARNING

Use $0.30M as the monthly early-warning threshold. Trigger mix, capacity and discretionary-spend actions before the buffer crosses it—not after breakeven is threatened.

05 / OPERATING SCALE & EBIT

Q1 converted revenue into EBIT best; Q3 Opex intensity peaked at 34.9%.

OPEX INTENSITY
EBIT SCALE
36.5% Q1 EBIT MARGIN
EVIDENCE / MONTHLY SCALE

Monthly Opex intensity rose from 24.6% in January to 39.3% in September, the same month net margin fell to 8.2%. Cost absorption weakened with demand.

EVIDENCE / QUARTERLY SCALE

Q1 generated $1.764M EBIT on $4.830M revenue; Q3 generated only $0.826M on $3.826M, cutting EBIT margin from 36.5% to 21.6%.

DECISION / OPEX GUARDRAIL

Benchmark the Q1 cost profile and require a named variance plan whenever monthly Opex exceeds 33% of revenue. Pair the ratio with EBIT so efficiency is not judged in isolation.

EXECUTIVE SYNTHESIS

Four connections define the financial operating system.

01 / PORTFOLIO × PROFIT

Sportswear is the economic engine.

Evidence: 38.8% of revenue created 63.4% of net profit, with a 40.2% net margin.

Management reading: allocate growth capital by earnings conversion; keep Nutrition behind a positive-contribution gate.

02 / SEASON × PORTFOLIO

September exposes weak lines.

Evidence: revenue fell to $1.121M, net margin to 8.2%, while two business lines posted losses.

Management reading: build a pre-September mix and cost plan; the trough is visible early enough to manage.

03 / COST × RESILIENCE

Concentration narrows the cushion.

Evidence: Labor is 66.9% of COGS; three pools are 74.5% of Opex; September cushion was $0.278M.

Management reading: put concentrated spend under owner-level productivity, utilization and ROI controls.

04 / OPEX × EBIT SCALE

Cost discipline determines scale.

Evidence: Q1 EBIT margin was 36.5%; Q3 fell to 21.6% as Opex intensity reached 34.9%.

Management reading: trigger a variance plan above 33% monthly Opex/revenue and track the EBIT consequence.
