Business background

Decision objective: distinguish productive reach from wasted pressure so campaign capacity moves toward higher-propensity audiences, channels and moments.

THE SIGNAL
Volume is not the objective. Conversion improves when timing, channel, audience and contact pressure align.
TIMING / MIX EFFECT
May carried 33.4% of contacts yet converted only 6.4%
6.4%MAY CONVERSION

Channel mix weakened

Only 40.1% of May contacts used cellular versus 63.5% overall; cellular converted at 11.1%, telephone at 3.3%.

Audience mix also mattered

Blue-collar prospects were 30.5% of May volume and converted at 5.4%. Rebalance mix before adding contacts.

CHANNEL / PRODUCTIVITY
Cellular converts at 14.7% versus 5.2% by telephone
83.0%CONVERSIONS VIA CELLULAR

Scale and productivity align

Cellular represented 63.5% of contacts and delivered 3,853 conversions.

Telephone needs proof

Telephone produced 787 conversions from 15,044 contacts. Retain it only where segment results justify the capacity.

AUDIENCE / PROPENSITY
Students convert at 31.4%; retirees at 25.2%
15.3%CONVERSION SHARE COMBINED

High propensity is specialized

Students generated 275 conversions from 875 contacts; retirees generated 434 from 1,720.

Scale plays a different role

Admin was the largest segment at 10,422 contacts but converted at 13.0%. Treat propensity and scale separately.

HISTORY / TARGETING SIGNAL
Prior-success audiences convert at 65.1%
894CONVERSIONS FROM PRIOR SUCCESS

History reshapes propensity

Prior-success prospects convert at 65.1%, versus 14.2% after prior failure and 8.8% with no history.

Small groups can carry strong signal

The prior-success audience contained 1,373 contacts. Use outcome history as a first-order targeting variable.

FREQUENCY / CONTACT PRESSURE
Conversion falls from 13.0% on attempt one to 3.1% at 11+
−9.9 ptsFREQUENCY DECAY

Early contacts carry the economics

17,642 first attempts converted at 13.0%; the 4–5 attempt band fell to 8.7%.

Persistence becomes waste

At 11+ attempts, 869 contacts converted at only 3.1%. Cap pressure and redirect capacity toward stronger signals.
