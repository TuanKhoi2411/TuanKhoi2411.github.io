BACKGROUND & DASHBOARD OBJECTIVE
Turn transaction data into commercial decisions

Business background

The workbook contains 541,909 invoice lines from 01 December 2010 through 09 December 2011. It connects sales, cancellations, products, customers and 38 countries, allowing gross demand to be reconciled to revenue retained.

Decision objective: identify where momentum is durable, which customer and market concentrations matter, and whether “returns” are true merchandise reversals or administrative adjustments.

Measurement guardrail: December 2011 is partial through 09 December. It is disclosed but not interpreted as a complete month.
PRIMARY AUDIENCE

Commercial leadership

Commercial Director, Sales and Category Managers, CRM and Customer Operations.

DECISION HORIZON

Weekly to seasonal

Trading reviews, peak planning, return exceptions and retention actions.

ANALYSIS SCOPE & LIMITS
  • Track complete-month momentum
  • Expose UK concentration
  • Separate known and anonymous sales
  • Classify admin vs product reversals
  • No margin or fulfilment cost available
  • No causal claim from transactions alone
