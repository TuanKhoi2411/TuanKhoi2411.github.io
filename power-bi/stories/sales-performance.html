Business background

This UK online-retail dataset captures invoice-level sales, cancellations, products, customers and countries across December 2010–December 2011. Strong seasonal demand coexists with returns, geographic concentration and uneven customer value.

Decision objective: identify where growth is durable, where value leaks, and which operating levers deserve attention before the next peak.

EXECUTIVE SYNTHESIS
Four connections redefine the sales operating system

Peak × Geography

November is the demand peak and 87.8% of its sales came from the UK—capacity risk is geographically concentrated.

Product × Customer

Product dependence is modest, yet repeat buyers create 93.1% of identified revenue—retention matters more than hero SKUs.

Demand × Returns

The UK supplies 84.0% of sales but 90.9% of return value—quality risk is more concentrated than demand.

Capacity × Retention

Peak execution, UK resilience and repeat-customer value must be managed as one system.
