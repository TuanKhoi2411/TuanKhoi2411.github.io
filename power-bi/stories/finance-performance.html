BACKGROUND & DASHBOARD OBJECTIVE
Read Apple growth through profitability and resilience

Business background

The dashboard uses Apple Inc. SEC EDGAR Company Facts from forms 10-K and 10-Q. It connects quarterly revenue and earnings with margin structure, operating costs, assets, liabilities and cash.

Decision objective: determine whether earnings momentum is structurally supported by margin discipline and balance-sheet capacity—not simply by top-line growth.

PRIMARY AUDIENCE
