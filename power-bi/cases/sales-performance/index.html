WHAT THIS DEMONSTRATES

From demand signal
to operating action.

The case is designed to show how a commercial review can connect growth, quality, and retention without losing the source trail.

01 / DECISION SUPPORT

Peak readiness

The dashboard frames November demand beside UK concentration and return behavior. That lets commercial leaders plan capacity without treating revenue as the only success measure.

02 / COMMERCIAL LOGIC

Quality before volume

Gross-to-net leakage, product breadth, and repeat-customer value are read together. The result separates durable demand from sales that create service or return pressure.

03 / GOVERNANCE

Traceable review

Measures, filters, source data, and limitations remain inspectable in the project folder. Reviewers can follow each headline back to the PBIX rather than relying on a static claim.

Preview note — This interactive dashboard is a presentation rebuild of the published Power BI report. The underlying figures are drawn from the real PBIX and cited UCI dataset.
