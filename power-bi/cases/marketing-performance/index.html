01 / DECISION SUPPORT

Audience prioritization

The review separates high-propensity groups from high-volume groups. Marketing can tailor treatment for students and retirees while managing admin contacts as a scale segment.

02 / CAMPAIGN LOGIC

Mix-aware conversion

Channel, timing, history, and frequency are connected rather than read in isolation. This explains why more contacts can still produce weaker conversion.

03 / GOVERNANCE

Explicit contact rules

The findings support channel thresholds, prior-outcome targeting, and a frequency ceiling. Those rules turn exploratory analysis into a repeatable campaign control process.
