WHAT THIS DEMONSTRATES

Turn the P&L
into a control system.

The case moves beyond consolidated profit to reveal which business lines, cost pools, and seasonal periods actually create or consume resilience.

01 / DECISION SUPPORT

Allocation by economics

Revenue and net profit are compared by business line, exposing Sportswear as the economic engine and Nutrition as a loss-making constraint. Funding decisions can follow margin quality rather than scale alone.

02 / FP&A LOGIC

Resilience under pressure

Monthly margin, break-even cushion, Opex intensity, and quarterly EBIT are connected. September becomes an explicit stress threshold instead of a surprise after close.

03 / GOVERNANCE

Named operating guardrails

The analysis supports margin-recovery gates, cost-pool ownership, and an Opex trigger above one-third of revenue. Those controls make recommendations monitorable in the next review cycle.
