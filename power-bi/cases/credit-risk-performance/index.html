02Dataset foundation

Grain · Coverage · Quality · Meaning

KNOW THE DATA BEFORE THE METRIC

One application.
One analytical grain.

The case uses a historical consumer-loan dataset supplied for the CP2 case study. Each row represents one loan application—not a payment transaction—so portfolio rates, borrower comparisons and recovery measures share a consistent denominator.

DATASET BRIEF / WHAT IS BEING ANALYSED

Borrowers, loan terms and observed outcomes.

This is a loan-level view of a consumer-credit portfolio. It connects who applied, what they borrowed, how the loan was priced and what happened through the reporting horizon.

Subject
Consumer-loan applicants and the loans recorded for them.
Observation
One unique loan ID equals one application-level record.
Data
Borrower profile, requested and funded amounts, term, purpose, grade, pricing, status, payments and recovery.
Business use
Locate portfolio risk, test pricing adequacy and focus recovery or review actions.
Boundary
Portfolio analysis—not an automated approval or rejection decision for an individual borrower.
