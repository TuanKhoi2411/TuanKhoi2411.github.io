WHAT THIS DEMONSTRATES

Connect risk,
pricing, and action.

The case makes model quality and portfolio economics visible together, so a high-risk segment can be evaluated through concentration, pricing, recovery, and operational response.

01 / PORTFOLIO

Risk concentration

Grade, term, purpose, state, income, and home-ownership cuts reveal where exposure and bad-loan behavior are concentrated.

02 / ECONOMICS

Pricing and recovery

Interest rate, expected return, outstanding principal, and recovery measures show whether compensation is aligned with risk.

03 / GOVERNANCE

Model action rules

AUC, feature importance, and rule combinations are presented with limits so model output supports—not replaces—credit judgment.

Portfolio note — This public-data case is an analytical demonstration, not a lending recommendation or production credit policy.
